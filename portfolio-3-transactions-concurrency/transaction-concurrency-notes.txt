Current isolated demonstration

Only the specific `p3_demo` schema is used by the existing scripts. Account 1 begins at 5,000.00, Account 2 at 2,500.00 and Account 3 at 100.00. The captured run went as follows: lost-update test, READ COMMITTED visibility test, committed transfer, insufficient-funds attempt and guarded updates. Before repeating the entire sequence, run the setup again.

Expected results for confirmation in pgAdmin:

- A committed transfer of 750.00 results in one completed ledger row and balances of 4,250.00 and 3,250.00.
- Attempt to deduct 750.00 from account 3 due to insufficient funds: PostgreSQL rejects the resulting -650.00 balance; rollback leaves account 3 at 100.00, account 2 at 3,250.00, and no failed transfer row.
- READ COMMITTED visibility: 3,350.00 is uncommitted in Session A; 3,250.00 in Session B and 3,250.00 remains after rollback.
- Unguarded stale read-then-write: after both withdrawals, the final balance is 4,150.00 rather than the correct 4,000.00; both sessions read 4,250.00.
- Guarded atomic updates: After waiting for Session A, both withdrawals are applied and the total balance is 3,900.00.

My recorded pgAdmin results showed these results. These isolated examples are coursework demonstrations rather than a production concurrency or load test; the screenshots are included in the P3 README.

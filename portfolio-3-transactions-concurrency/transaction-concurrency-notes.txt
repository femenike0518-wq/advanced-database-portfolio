## Current isolated demonstration

The current scripts use only the dedicated `p3_demo` schema. The setup starts account 1 at 5,000.00, account 2 at 2,500.00 and account 3 at 100.00. The captured run proceeded sequentially: committed transfer, insufficient-funds attempt, READ COMMITTED visibility test, lost-update test, then guarded updates. Rerun the setup before repeating the full sequence.

Expected results to verify in pgAdmin:

- Committed transfer of 750.00: balances become 4,250.00 and 3,250.00, with one completed ledger row.
- Insufficient-funds attempt to debit 750.00 from account 3: PostgreSQL rejects the resulting -650.00 balance; rollback leaves account 3 at 100.00, account 2 at 3,250.00, and no failed transfer row.
- READ COMMITTED visibility: Session A sees its uncommitted 3,350.00; Session B sees 3,250.00; rollback leaves 3,250.00.
- Unguarded stale read-then-write: both sessions read 4,250.00; final balance is 4,150.00 instead of the correct 4,000.00 after both withdrawals.
- Guarded atomic updates: Session B waits for Session A, then both withdrawals apply and the final balance is 3,900.00.

These outcomes were observed in my captured pgAdmin results. The screenshots are listed in the P3 README; these isolated examples are coursework demonstrations, not a production concurrency or load test.

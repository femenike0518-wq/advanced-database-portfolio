Small-table experiment

Prior to and following the balance index, the three-account SwiftBank experiment recorded roughly 0.108 and 0.133 milliseconds, respectively. Sequential scans were used by PostgreSQL in the saved plans. This timing difference cannot establish a general indexing effect because the dataset is too small. Only the exercise index is dropped and recreated by the repeatable script; sample rows are left unchanged.

Validation of a larger synthetic benchmark

Environment: My Windows computer is running PostgreSQL 18.6 and pgAdmin 4. The SwiftBank sample tables were separated from the benchmark schema.

Rows loaded: 50,000 customers, 100,000 accounts and 500,000 transactions. Prior to and following the creation of indexes, four queries were measured. After one warm-up, each median employs seven measured runs. Hashes and baseline/indexed row counts matched.

Query | Baseline median ms | Indexed median ms | Shared buffer hits, baseline to indexed | Plan observation
Customer-account lookup | 11.878 | 0.045 | 938 to 6 | Accounts Seq Scan became an Index Scan using p2_accounts_customer_id_idx.
Account-type aggregate | 108.407 | 43.528 | 935 to 935 | Sequential Scan remained in both plans; the timing difference is not attributed to the index.
Account transaction history | 126.342 | 0.043 | 10,728 to 8 | Parallel Seq Scans became Index Only Scans using source/destination history indexes.
One-week date report | 106.458 | 4.749 | 5,336 to 72 | Parallel Seq Scan became an Index Only Scan using p2_transactions_date_report_idx.

In these warm-cache measurements, there were zero shared read blocks. Despite having a lower measured median, the date-range query touched a little more shared buffers after indexing. These local results are not production guarantees and rely on this machine, data and query distribution.

Following creation, the following index sizes were recorded: p2_accounts_customer_id_idx 1,992 kB; p2_accounts_type_balance_idx 3,816 kB; p2_transactions_destination_history_idx 24 MB; p2_transactions_source_history_idx 24 MB; and p2_transactions_date_report_idx 28 MB. These indexes make use of storage and increase the effort required for updates and inserts.

The evidence for the entire pgAdmin plan is stored in evidence/. Paired screenshots with a -b continuation are used in the baseline history, baseline date-range, indexed customer lookup and indexed history plans. Although its plan remained a sequential scan in both stages, the account-type aggregate median was lower in the indexed stage; the index is not responsible for the timing discrepancy. Shared read blocks were zero; these are not production guarantees, but rather warm-cache results from a single local machine.
